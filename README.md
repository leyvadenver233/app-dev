# My Favorite Series: Attack on Titan

## Overview
**Attack on Titan** (_Shingeki no Kyojin_) is a Japanese anime series based on the manga by Hajime Isayama.

> *"If you win, you live. If you lose, you die. If you don't fight, you can't win!"*  
> — Eren Yeager

---

## Main Characters

- **Eren Yeager** - The main protagonist who vows to destroy all Titans.
- **Mikasa Ackerman** - Eren's adoptive sister and one of the strongest fighters in the Survey Corps.
- **Armin Arlert** - Eren’s best friend, known for his intelligence and strategy.
- **Levi Ackerman** - Humanity's strongest soldier.

---

## Why I Like It

1. **Epic Storyline** - The story keeps you on the edge of your seat.
2. **Complex Characters** - Every character has depth and a meaningful backstory.
3. **Mind-blowing Plot Twists** - The plot constantly evolves in unpredictable ways.
4. **Stunning Animation** 🎥
